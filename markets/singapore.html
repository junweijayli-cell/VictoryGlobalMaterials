Singapore · Requirements and documentation

Procurement enquiries for Singapore projects

We welcome discussions with Singapore contractors and procurement teams about project requirements and potential China-sourcing routes. This page is a procurement enquiry guide; it does not identify any catalogue fitting as eligible for supply or installation in Singapore.

Discuss procurement requirements

Define the purchasing and application requirements

  • Identify the actual purchasing company, application and specification, including whether the requirement involves potable water.
  • Confirm whether procurement permits an overseas contracting party or requires a local supplier or importer.
  • Victory Global Materials is an independent trading company registered in China. No Singapore office, warehouse, local stock or established local supply arrangement is claimed.

Evidence before product eligibility claims

  • PUB states that water fittings for water-service installations must be tested to its stipulated standards before they are supplied, displayed, offered or advertised for that use.
  • No selected-model evidence establishing Singapore water-service eligibility has been supplied for this catalogue. We do not present it as an approved Singapore range.
  • Share the project specification and document checklist. Manufacturer, model, test reports and applicable requirements must be verified before a product-specific supply proposal.

Clarify the commercial route before quotation

  • Identify the contracting party, importer and intended delivery point.
  • Review packaging, shipping, destination handling, customs, unloading and delivery responsibilities for the actual goods and route.
  • Prices, availability, payment dates and transport timing require confirmation. A discussion or document review is not an offer of an unverified fitting for Singapore use.

Buyer questions

Singapore procurement questions

Are the catalogue products PUB-approved?

We do not claim PUB approval or confirmed Singapore water-service eligibility for the catalogue. Product-specific evidence is required; a general ISO statement or a catalogue logo does not establish it.

What should I include in an initial enquiry?

Share the application, project specification, dimensions, quantities and document requirements, together with the purchasing company and destination. Identify potable-water use explicitly.

Are you a Singapore-based supplier?

Victory Global Materials is registered in China. No Singapore company, warehouse or local stock is represented here; any required local purchasing route must be confirmed.

Can you quote immediately from a catalogue?

A catalogue is a starting reference. Product eligibility, documents, purchasing arrangements and delivery scope must be verified before a product-specific commercial proposal.
