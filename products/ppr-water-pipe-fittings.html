Buyer questions

Questions about PP-R Water Pipe & Fittings

Does the catalogue pressure apply at every water temperature?

The existing catalogue lists up to 2.5 MPa, but it does not supply a model-specific pressure-temperature table. Request that table for the chosen pipe series and operating conditions before specifying the system.

Can pipes and fittings be quoted together?

This product family includes pipes and fittings such as valves, elbows, tees and reducers. Include a fittings schedule and quantities with your enquiry so the whole system can be reviewed.
