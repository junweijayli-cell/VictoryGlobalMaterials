Buyer questions

Questions about PVC Cable Trunking & Electrical Conduit

How do I choose between trunking and conduit for an enquiry?

The catalogue lists trunking for surface routes and conduit for concealed routes. Provide your cable layout, installation method and required dimensions so the appropriate listed components can be reviewed.

Is a UL94 V-0 rating confirmed for the entire range?

Fire performance must be confirmed for the selected model. Request a test report covering UL94 V-0 or the classification required by your project, including the tested product and the report's scope.
