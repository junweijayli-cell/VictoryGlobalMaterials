Buyer questions

Questions about HDPE Gas Pipe

Why are pressure and temperature ratings requested separately?

Confirm the operating limits against the selected manufacturer's gas-pipe approval and rating table. Obtain the product documentation and project approval before specification, with the gas application, grade and SDR clearly identified.

What information should a gas-pipe enquiry include?

Include the gas application, grade, SDR or required pressure class, dimensions, quantity and destination, plus the project specification. Ask for the approval and jointing documentation associated with the quoted product.
