How enquiries work

From Requirements
to a Reviewed Quotation

A catalogue is the start of a sourcing discussion. Product suitability, documents and commercial arrangements need to be checked for the actual project.

We aim to build long-term relationships through clear requirements and documented quotations. Enquiries from other Southeast Asian destinations are welcome for review.

1 · Define the requirement
Identify the buyer, application, material, connection method, dimensions and quantities.
2 · Review products and documents
Match the specification to a selected manufacturer and check the required evidence.
3 · Assess packaging and delivery
Review the destination, packed dimensions, handling, importer and delivery responsibilities.
4 · Confirm the quotation scope
Agree the product, documents, price basis, delivery scope and payment terms before a commitment.
5 · Proceed on confirmed terms
Establish purchasing and supply arrangements for the agreed order; stock, timing and local acceptance are not assumed.
